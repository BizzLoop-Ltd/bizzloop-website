Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026 · BIZZLOOP LTD (Company No: 17319641)

BIZZLOOP LTD ("BizzLoop", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with the Data Protection Act 2018 and UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR).

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide voluntarily when booking demonstrations, requesting consultations, applying for career opportunities, or registering as a partner:

  • Contact details (Name, Work Email, Telephone/WhatsApp Number)
  • Business details (Company Name, Team Size, Operational Preferences)
  • Candidate information (CV/Resume files, Employment History)

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information strictly to provide our services, configure software instances, schedule live walkthroughs, and communicate important updates regarding your account.

3. Data Ownership & Security

You retain full ownership of all customer records, contact databases, and operational data uploaded to your BizzLoop instance. We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to any third party.

4. Your Statutory Rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to access, rectify, or request deletion of your personal data held by us. Contact our Data Governance Lead at info@bizzloop.co.uk.
