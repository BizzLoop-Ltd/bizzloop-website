Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026 · BIZZLOOP LTD (Company No. 17319641)

BIZZLOOP LTD ("BizzLoop", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, process, store, and safeguard personal data when you visit our website at bizzloop.co.uk, inquire about our software, or subscribe to our Business Operating System services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information directly from you when you interact with our website or services. This includes:

  • Contact Information: Full name, work email address, telephone/WhatsApp number, job title, and business name when submitting demo booking or contact forms.
  • Business Details: Industry category, estimated staff count, and specific operational requirements submitted during consultations.
  • Career Applications: Resume/CV details, portfolio links, employment background, and contact information submitted via our Careers application forms.
  • Technical & Usage Data: Anonymised IP addresses, browser type, operating system, pages viewed, and referral sources collected automatically for performance monitoring.

2. How We Use Personal Data

We process your personal information strictly for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Scheduling and delivering product demonstration calls and technical consultations.
  • Setting up, configuring, and supporting your BizzLoop Business Operating System subscription.
  • Responding to your inquiries, customer support requests, and billing communications.
  • Assessing candidate suitability for open career and internship positions.
  • Maintaining website security, detecting fraud, and ensuring continuous platform stability.

3. Lawful Bases for Processing

Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), we rely on the following lawful bases:

  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a)): When you voluntarily submit forms, request demo consultations, or opt-in to marketing communications.
  • Contract Performance (Article 6(1)(b)): Necessary to enter into or perform our service agreement with your business.
  • Legitimate Interests (Article 6(1)(f)): To operate, secure, and improve our services, provided your rights do not override these interests.

4. Cookies & Similar Technologies

Our website uses essential technical cookies required for website security, session management, and responsive functionality. We do not place non-essential advertising or invasive third-party tracking cookies without user consent, adhering strictly to ICO Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR) guidelines.

5. Data Sharing & Third-Party Processors

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data to third parties for marketing purposes. Data may be shared only with trusted infrastructure service providers (e.g. secure UK/EU cloud hosting, Google Apps Script webhooks for internal CRM processing) operating under strict data processing agreements.

6. Data Retention & Security

All personal data is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS protocols. We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, or to comply with statutory accounting and legal obligations (typically up to 6 years for business financial records).

7. Your UK Data Protection Rights

Under UK data protection law, you hold rights including:

  • Right of Access: Request copies of your personal data (Subject Access Request).
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request deletion of your personal data under certain conditions.
  • Right to Restriction & Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests or request restricted processing.

8. Contact Us & ICO Complaint Rights

To exercise any of your data protection rights, please email us at info@bizzloop.co.uk. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk if you believe your data has been handled unlawfully.

Compliance Framework

UK GDPR Compliance Statement

Last Updated: August 2026 · BIZZLOOP LTD (Company No. 17319641)

BIZZLOOP LTD is committed to protecting personal data and maintaining robust, transparent data protection practices in accordance with applicable UK data protection law, including the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Principles of Data Protection

In accordance with ICO guidance, BizzLoop adheres to core data protection principles:

  • Lawfulness, Fairness & Transparency: Personal data is processed fairly, lawfully, and in an open manner.
  • Purpose Limitation: Collected solely for specified, explicit, and legitimate operational purposes.
  • Data Minimisation: Processing is adequate, relevant, and limited to what is strictly necessary.
  • Accuracy: Data is kept accurate and up to date where reasonably necessary.
  • Storage Limitation: Data is retained no longer than necessary for processing purposes.
  • Integrity & Confidentiality: Technical encryption and physical security protect against unauthorized access.

2. Data Protection by Design & Default

BizzLoop integrates data protection by design and default into system architecture, evaluating data privacy from the initial design phase of all software features, database schemas, and customer lead forms.

3. Handling Data Subject Access Requests (DSAR)

Individuals can submit Subject Access Requests, correction requests, or erasure requests by emailing info@bizzloop.co.uk. Requests are responded to within one calendar month in accordance with statutory guidelines.

4. Data Breach Management

We maintain an active Data Breach Policy. In the event of a personal data breach posing a risk to individuals, BizzLoop will notify the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach.
