Frequently Asked Pricing Questions

Everything you need to know about our subscriptions and UK onboarding.

Are there any long-term contract lock-ins?

No. Monthly subscriptions can be adjusted or cancelled with 30 days notice. Annual plans provide a 16.7% discount (2 months free) in exchange for an annual commitment.

How does onboarding and workspace setup work?

Our UK technical team assists with instance configuration, user account creation, lead intake form wiring, and staff walkthrough calls to ensure a smooth launch.

Can we upgrade our plan as our team grows?

Yes. You can upgrade from Starter to Growth or Enterprise instantly without any downtime or data migration headaches.
