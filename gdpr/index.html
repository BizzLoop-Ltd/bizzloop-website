UK GDPR Statement

Last Updated: August 2026 · BIZZLOOP LTD (Company No: 17319641)

BIZZLOOP LTD is fully committed to upholding the data protection principles defined under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR):

  • Lawfulness, Fairness & Transparency: Personal data is processed fairly and transparently with explicit consent.
  • Purpose Limitation: Data is collected solely for declared business demonstration, operational management, or recruitment purposes.
  • Data Minimisation: We request only the data strictly necessary to fulfill our service commitments.
  • Integrity & Confidentiality: All client data is encrypted in transit and at rest in certified UK/EU cloud data centres.
