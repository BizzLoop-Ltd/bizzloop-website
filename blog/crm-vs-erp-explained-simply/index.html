Business software jargon can be confusing. Many small business owners hear "CRM" and "ERP" and assume these are expensive, complex systems reserved exclusively for massive multinational corporations. In reality, every single business performs CRM and ERP activities every day.

What is CRM? (Front-Office Growth)

CRM (Customer Relationship Management) focuses on the relationship before and during the sale. It manages:

  • Inbound enquiries from websites, phone calls, and referrals
  • Deal pipelines and quotation follow-ups
  • Customer contact records, notes, and conversation history
  • Marketing email sequences and review collection

What is ERP? (Back-Office Delivery)

ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) focuses on operational fulfillment after the sale is agreed. It manages:

  • Invoicing, payment collection, and UK VAT calculations
  • Inventory, stock movement, and supplier purchasing
  • Staff scheduling, job allocation, and project tasks
  • Financial ledgers and executive profit reporting

The Power of Connecting Them in BizzLoop

When CRM and ERP operate in separate software packages, your team becomes the "human bridge" manually copying data between systems. BizzLoop connects front-office growth with back-office execution so your entire company operates from one source of truth.
